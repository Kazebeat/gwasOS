Interoperability Landscape 2024
June 2025
The report explains how blockchain interoperability has shifted from hack-prone “lock-and-mint” bridges to decentralized messaging protocols that securely pass data between chains, enabling everything from asset transfers to more complex cross-chain applications. It compares the leading protocols (LayerZero, Wormhole, Axelar), discusses how incentives/airdrops and tokenomics affect their apparent traction and valuation, and highlights “intents” as a potential next wave that lets users specify outcomes while solvers compete to execute the best route.
